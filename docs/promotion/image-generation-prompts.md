# DistroNexus v2.0.1 题图生成提示词 (Gemini/Midjourney/DALL-E)

**Goal**: 生成一张现代、科技感的题图，用于 DistroNexus v2.0.1 发布文章。这张图需要体现 "WSL 管理"、".NET/WPF 现代化"、"AI 协同" 和 "工程化/标准化" 的概念。

---

## Option 1: 抽象科技风 (Abstract Tech - 推荐)

**Prompt (English):**
> A futuristic, isometric 3D illustration representing a modern Windows desktop development environment focused on Linux integration.
> Central element: A glowing, translucent glass cube containing a stylized Linux penguin logo, seamlessly integrated into a sleek specific Windows 11 style glass panel interface.
> Surrounding the cube are floating holographic code snippets (C#, PowerShell) and blueprint schematics representing "Architecture", "Templates", and "Automation".
> Color palette: Deep obsidian black background with vibrant neon accents in Microsoft Blue (#0078D4) and WSL Orange (#E95420).
> Incorporate subtle "AI" network nodes connecting the elements, symbolizing AI-collaborative development.
> Style: High-tech, clean, professional, cinematic lighting, 8k resolution, verifying "DistroNexus" text is NOT included (or minimal abstract text).

**设计意图**:
- **玻璃立方体 + Linux 企鹅**: 象征 WSL (Windows Subsystem for Linux) 的容器化与集成。
- **Windows 11 玻璃面板**: 代表 v2.0.1 的原生 WPF UI 和 Fluent Design。
- **全息代码/蓝图**: 代表工程化、标准化和模板系统。
- **AI 节点**: 隐喻 AI 协同编程的背景。
- **配色**: 微软蓝 + Ubuntu 橙，经典的 WSL 配色组合。

---

## Option 2: 极简扁平风 (Minimalist Flat - 适合作为 Banner)

**Prompt (English):**
> A clean, minimalist flat vector illustration for a software release banner.
> Split composition: Left side shows a stylized, simplified Windows window frame with a modern dashboard layout (dark mode). Right side shows a structured array of colorful modular blocks (representing templates and distros) flowing into the window.
> A robotic arm or an abstract AI spark is gently arranging the blocks, symbolizing AI assistance and automation.
> Colors: Dark grey background, with white, blue, and orange flat elements.
> Text "v2.0" can be subtly integrated into the background geometry if the AI handles text well, otherwise keep text-free.
> Style: Corporate tech blog style, vector art, Notion-style illustration but more detailed.

**设计意图**:
- **窗口 + 模块流**: 直观展示"模版化"和"自动化"如何流入开发环境。
- **AI 元素**: 强调 AI 在构建过程中的作用。
- **扁平风格**: 适合在公众号/博客作为不抢眼的题图。

---

## Option 3: 赛博朋克/开发极客风 (Cyberpunk/Dev Geek)

**Prompt (English):**
> A digital art scene of a developer's desk setup at night.
> On the monitor: A crisp, modern dashboard interface labeled "DistroNexus" (or abstract UI) managing multiple Linux terminal windows.
> From the screen, digital roots or connections flow out into a physical-looking 3D space, forming a structured grid of server racks and cloud icons.
> An ethereal, glowing AI avatar or pair of virtual hands is hovering over the keyboard, "co-typing" code.
> Lighting: Moody purple and cyan ambient lighting, with the screen casting a warm orange glow (WSL colors) on the desk.
> Style: Cyberpunk, vaporwave aesthetic, highly detailed, realistic textures.

**设计意图**:
- **开发者桌面**: 建立代入感，"这是给开发者用的工具"。
- **虚拟手/AI**: 具象化"AI 协同编程"的概念。
- **屏幕溢出**: 象征软件能力延伸到现实工作流。

---

## 建议

- **首选 Option 1**：最符合 v2.0.1 "重构"、"现代化"、"架构升级" 的宏大叙事感。
- 生成后，可以在图片中央叠加文字： **DistroNexus v2.0** (大标题) 和 **AI-Driven WSL Manager** (小标题)。
